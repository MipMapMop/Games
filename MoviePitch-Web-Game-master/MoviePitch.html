🎬 Movie Pitch

One player becomes the Producer. The Producer receives a movie Genre. Everyone else receives Character and Plot Twist cards. Create a movie using:

1 Character + 1 Plot Twist

Pitch your movie idea to the Producer. The Producer chooses the best movie. The winner earns a point. The Producer role rotates each round.

The player with the most points wins!
